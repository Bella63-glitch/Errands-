Why I started RME

The idea for RME came from noticing problems that kept coming up in different places.

People needed errands done in other towns, but finding someone they could trust wasn't always easy. I saw people lose money to scams simply because they couldn't verify a shop or the person they were dealing with from another city.

At the same time, there were young people looking for work and struggling to find reliable ways to earn an income.

Businesses had a different challenge. Finding dependable people to handle deliveries and everyday operational tasks was harder than it needed to be.

I started thinking about how these problems could be addressed through one platform.

What if someone in Nairobi could confidently get something done in Eldoret without having to travel? What if a young person could earn through providing services? What if a business could find reliable people to handle its deliveries without constantly worrying about whether the job would get done?

That was the thinking behind RME.

We've been building on that idea, growing our provider network and working with individuals and businesses across Kenya. There's still a lot to improve and a lot more I want RME to become.

I want us to build a platform people can genuinely depend on, whether they're requesting an errand, looking for an opportunity to earn or running a business that needs reliable support.

That's why we started, and that's what we're working towards.

Christabellah MurichoFounder, RME

How we keep customers and providers safe

Earning on RME comes with responsibility, and customers need to feel safe. Every provider goes through onboarding before taking on work.

  1. ID and reference checksWe check each provider's identity and speak to their references.
  2. Provider agreementProviders sign a service provider agreement and receive a handbook explaining what is expected of them.
  3. TrainingProviders are trained before they start, including how to communicate with customers.
  4. A clear complaints processIf someone reports mistreatment, we follow a defined procedure to handle it.
