About RME

Connecting people, businesses and local service providers.

RME is a technology-enabled local services marketplace designed to make it easier for people and businesses to access the services they need.

We bring customers and service providers together through one platform, helping simplify how local services are discovered, requested and delivered.

Local services.
Better connected.
01Our story

Built around a simple problem: getting things done shouldn't be difficult.

Everyday tasks often require finding someone reliable, coordinating the work and making sure the job gets completed.

For businesses, these small tasks can become operational challenges.

For individuals, they can take time away from more important things.

RME was created to make these connections easier.

What started with addressing everyday service needs has grown into a marketplace connecting customers and providers across multiple counties in Kenya.
02What we do

RME connects demand for local services with a network of service providers.
