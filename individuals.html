How RME works

Getting an errand done is simple.

  1. 1

    Tell us what you need

    Describe the errand, provide the location and include any important instructions.

  2. 2

    Submit your request

    Post your errand through the RME platform and receive a reference number.

  3. 3

    Get connected to a provider

    Your request becomes available to eligible service providers who can choose to accept it.

  4. 4

    Get it done

    Once a provider accepts, you can coordinate with them to complete your errand.
